Corporate Affairs Commission Information Guide

Understanding business structures and compliance requirements in Nigeria.

What is the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC)?

The Corporate Affairs Commission was established under the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) to regulate the formation and management of companies in Nigeria. It is the sole government agency responsible for officially registering any legal business or corporate body in the country.

Operating a commercial business or organization without official CAC integration is illegal under Nigerian corporate regulation framework policies. Registration guarantees brand identity protection and legal operational status.

💼

Business Name Registration

Best suited for sole proprietors, artisans, freelancers, and small partnerships. It is fast to register and ideal for businesses managed directly under an owner's personal legal liability framework.

🏢

Private Limited Company (LTD)

Creates a distinct legal entity separate from its owners. Protects shareholders through limited liability rules. Required for corporate bidding, opening commercial bank accounts, and seeking investors.

🤝

Non-Governmental Organization (NGO)

Designed for non-profit organizations, charities, and social enterprises. Registration ensures legal recognition, enabling access to grants, donations, and tax exemptions.
